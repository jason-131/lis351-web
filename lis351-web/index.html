About the guide

Small directions can make a big difference

The guide is organized around three simple ideas: know what you need, find a reliable starting point, and ask for help when you need it.

Start with a question

Identify the problem before searching so you can choose the resource that fits your situation.

Use trusted resources

Look for official campus pages, service desks, libraries, and people who can point you in the right direction.

Share what works

A useful tip can help another student feel less stuck, especially when it is clear and easy to follow.

Where to begin

When you are unsure where to go, try one of these common starting points before searching the whole internet.

  • Academic questions: visit an advising or tutoring center
  • Research questions: ask a librarian for help
  • Well-being questions: contact student support services
